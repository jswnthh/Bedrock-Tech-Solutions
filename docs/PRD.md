# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Project Overview

Develop a professional corporate website for an IT infrastructure solutions and services business.

- **Project Name:** Bedrock Tech Solutions
- **Brand Name:** Bedrock Tech Solutions
- **Project Status:** Initial Development
- **Document Version:** 0.1.0
- **Created:** 2026-11-24
- **Last Updated:** 2026-11-24

## 2. Objectives

- Establish a professional online presence.
- Showcase the company's services, products and projects.
- Build credibility and trust among potential customers.
- Generate customer enquiries and quotation requests.
- Provide a simple way to manage website content.

The primary objective is to build a coherent, functional and maintainable website rather than a feature-heavy or technically complex platform.

## 3. Target Audience

- Businesses and corporate organizations
- Educational institutions
- Small and medium enterprises
- Offices and IT administrators
- Individuals seeking IT infrastructure solutions

## 4. Website Pages

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Home | Introduce the business and its offerings |
| About Us | Present company information |
| Services | Showcase available services |
| IT Solutions | Present business-specific solutions |
| Products | Display hardware and equipment |
| Projects | Showcase completed projects |
| Clients | Display clients and testimonials |
| Request a Quote | Collect customer requirements |
| Contact | Provide contact information and enquiry form |

## 5. Core Services

- Custom PC Building & Workstation Solutions
- Network Infrastructure Setup
- Server Installation & Configuration
- Windows & Linux Server Administration
- Active Directory & Domain Controller Setup
- Firewall, Router & Managed Switch Configuration
- Enterprise Wi-Fi Solutions
- CCTV & Network Security Solutions
- NAS, RAID & Data Backup Solutions
- IT Infrastructure Design & Implementation
- Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)
- IT Support & Troubleshooting

## 6. Functional Requirements

### Public Website

- Browse services, solutions and products.
- View completed projects and testimonials.
- Submit customer enquiries.
- Request quotations.
- Contact the business through WhatsApp and email.

### Admin Dashboard

- Secure administrator login.
- Manage services and products.
- Manage projects and testimonials.
- View customer enquiries and quotation requests.
- Update essential business information.

## 7. Design Requirements

- Clean, modern and professional appearance.
- Consistent typography, colors and components.
- Simple and intuitive navigation.
- Responsive mobile and desktop layouts.
- Subtle, purposeful animations.
- Clear calls to action.
- Readable and accessible content.

Detailed visual guidelines shall be maintained in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

## 8. Technical Requirements

- Reasonable loading speed.
- Basic SEO optimization.
- Secure handling of customer information.
- Reliable form submissions and email notifications.
- Reusable components.
- Maintainable and understandable code.
- Compatibility with modern browsers.

Technical implementation shall be defined in `ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## 9. Scope Limitations

The initial version does not require:

- E-commerce and online payments.
- Customer accounts.
- Advanced CRM functionality.
- Automated quotation generation.
- Inventory management.
- Complex animations or integrations.

Additional features may be introduced when a genuine business need arises.

## 10. Acceptance Criteria

The initial version is complete when:

- All required pages are functional.
- Navigation is consistent across pages.
- The website works on mobile and desktop.
- Enquiry and quotation forms work correctly.
- Administrators can manage essential content.
- There are no major functional or security issues.
- The website is ready for deployment.

## 11. Pending Decisions

- Brand name and logo
- Business contact information
- Final content and photographs
- Technology stack
- Hosting and domain
- Budget and timeline

## 12. Development Principles

- Prefer simplicity over complexity.
- Prioritize functionality over perfection.
- Maintain consistency throughout the website.
- Avoid premature optimization and unnecessary abstractions.
- Do not introduce features without a clear purpose.
- Use placeholders for unavailable content without fabricating business information.
- Build incrementally and improve through feedback.

## 13. Document Versioning

This PRD is a living document and may evolve throughout the development process.

- Major version: Significant changes to product scope or direction.
- Minor version: New or modified requirements.
- Patch version: Corrections, clarifications and minor documentation updates.

Update the document version, last updated date and revision history whenever meaningful changes are made.
